# Life360 Product Handover

**Reference date:** 25 November 2026  
**Workspace:** OpenTrack360

Life360 is a family safety and coordination app. Its main screen brings together people who share their location, pets, and tracked belongings. The product combines free location and safety features, paid benefits for a Circle, separately sold trackers, and services delivered with partners. This handover covers publicly documented capabilities, with the international site as the starting point. Availability can depend on the country, Circle membership, device, age, and rollout. ## Driving, SOS and emergency response

- **Drive Detection and Analysis.** The app uses phone location and motion data to distinguish drives from other trips, record routes and detect eligible driving events. Arity powers the related analysis. Disconnecting Arity disables Drive Detection and its associated reporting and crash features. The usual drive conditions include enough battery, background operation, a strong mobile signal and battery saver off. Events that miss drive criteria can appear as trips. [Drive Detection and Analysis](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053499870487-Drive-Detection-Analysis) · [Driving speed and accuracy](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053597039511-Driving-Speed-Accuracy)
- **Family Driving Summary and Individual Driver Reports.** The weekly family summary gives Circle-wide drive and behaviour totals. Gold and Platinum add member-level trip and behaviour details, including miles driven, top speed, phone use, speeding, hard braking and rapid acceleration when detected. Members can label a drive as a passenger trip. That change preserves its data in the weekly reports. [Weekly Family Driving Summary](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053618281239-Weekly-Family-Driving-Summary) · [Individual Driver Reports](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23292607876887-Individual-Driver-Reports) · [Driver or passenger](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053640152215-Change-from-Driver-to-Passenger)
- **Personalized auto insurance quotes.** Through a separate opt-in, eligible members can let Arity use their own driving behaviour to inform offers from participating insurers. The driving score is per person. Members can opt out of future use for the program. [Arity insurance quotes](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053397958551-Arity)
- **Phone SOS.** A member can start a silent alert with a short cancellation countdown. Circle members can receive the last detected location by notification, with SMS and calls dependent on market and tier. Emergency contacts are set per Circle and must accept the invitation. A compatible, configured Tile can initiate the same SOS flow. The Apple Watch version sends a Limited SOS to the Circle. [SOS Alerts](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053474049687-SOS-Alerts) · [Product and Services Terms, SOS and Tile SOS](https://legal.corp.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/17865862545815-Life360-Product-and-Services-Terms)
- **Crash Detection.** The phone can detect some serious collisions and notify the Circle. Eligible plans add a dispatch workflow that tries to reach the affected member and can contact local responders if needed. Support lists enabled Drive Detection and crash alerts, cellular signal, over 10% battery, battery saver off, relevant background location, motion and notification permissions, and approximately 25 mph in the five seconds before impact. Detection can miss collisions. The terms describe global basic mobile detection, while an older disclosure lists specific countries. Basic detection in any unlisted market, including India, remains unverified here. [Free Crash Detection](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053410919063-Life360-Free-Crash-Detection) · [Crash Detection disclosure](https://legal.corp.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/16044048651031-Crash-Detection-and-Emergency-Dispatch-Services) · [Product and Services Terms](https://legal.corp.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/17865862545815-Life360-Product-and-Services-Terms)

## Trackers, pets and hardware

### Tile for belongings

- **Bluetooth trackers.** Tile Pro, Mate, Slim and Sticker suit different objects, form factors and ranges. Find a Tile nearby with its ring or the proximity meter. A compatible Tile can ring the paired phone. Away from Bluetooth range, the map shows a last-known or network-reported location when a participating device detects it. Tile relies on Bluetooth sightings rather than continuous GPS. [How Tile works](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/30583028539799-How-Tile-Works) · [Tile models](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/30583020129047-Tile-Models)
- **Life360 integration.** Activate or add compatible Tile hardware, or connect an existing Tile account. The app supports Circle visibility, hiding and managing Tiles, recent item location history, low-battery alerts on applicable models, and near-range finding. Compatible 2024 models can be configured for discreet SOS. [Life360 and Tile](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053717327255-Life360-and-Tile) · [Tile in the Life360 app](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/sections/30603683543959-Tile)

## Platforms, accounts and controls

- **Mobile and web.** Current documented minimums are iOS 18 on iPhone and Android 10. The mobile app is unsupported on tablets, desktop computers, CarPlay, Android Auto and Android watches. The web provides public pages, account and supported membership flows, and temporary location link viewing. A new phone login signs out the previous phone. [Compatible devices](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053708235159-Life360-Compatible-Devices-and-Device-OS-Requirements) · [Multiple devices](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/23053659701655-Life360-on-Multiple-Devices) · [Temporary browser sharing](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/39407118334103-Temporary-Location-Sharing)
- **Apple Watch.** The standalone watch app is a limited U.S. rollout on compatible Apple Watches with cellular service. An adult manager links a separate managed member account. The wearer can share location, see Circle members, send Quick Notes and trigger Circle-only Limited SOS. Watch location updates may be less frequent. Place Alerts about the wearer go to Circle members’ phones. Watch-only use excludes crash detection and professional dispatch. - **Setup and reliability.** Signup covers account verification, date of birth and, where required, guardian approval for minors. Members grant the permissions that power each feature. Background and precise location, motion or physical activity, push, mobile data and Bluetooth matter for different functions.
